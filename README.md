# 🐰 Rabbit Med Dosages

**[Live site → julia-rabbit.surge.sh](http://julia-rabbit.surge.sh)**  
_A simple calculator to determine safe medication dosages for rabbits based on their weight._

---

## About the Project

This tool was built for my friend **Julia**, who devotes her life to rescuing rabbits — including abused pets, abandoned outdoor strays, and animals in critical need of care. She’s one of those people who puts everyone else (including rabbits) first.

She said, "I hate math." I wanted to help.

So I built this app — a lightweight calculator to simplify one of the most stressful parts of her work: figuring out accurate medication doses based on each rabbit's weight.

---

## Features

- Enter rabbit weight in **pounds**
- See safe dosage ranges for popular rabbit medications
- Built for mobile and web — usable right at the rescue site or vet

---

## Tech Stack

- HTML / CSS / JavaScript
- No frameworks or libraries — fast and simple
- Deployed via Surge

---

## Why This Matters

Proper dosing is life-saving. This app reduces the risk of error and makes Julia’s work just a little easier — which means more rabbits get help faster.
