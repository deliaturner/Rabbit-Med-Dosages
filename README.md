## Rabbit-Med-Dosages

julia-rabbit.surge.sh

Calculator for popular rabbit meds by weight (pounds)

This was created for my friend Julia, who donates her entire life to rescue rabbits. She takes in rabbits who have been abused, who haven't received proper care, and outdoor strays. Needless to say, she needs to be able to calculate dosages of various medicines for them. She said, "I hate math." So, I helped her.
